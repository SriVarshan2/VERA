--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
💡 WHY VERA USES SOFT CONFIDENCE WEIGHTING INSTEAD OF HARD EXCLUSION:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HARD EXCLUSION DISCARDS VALUABLE SIGNAL:
   Excluding flagged judges entirely (e.g. Marcus Vance or Elena Rostova) discarded
   66% of the judging data for this event. Even a judge who experienced late fatigue
   or exhibited low variance still provides valid ordinal feedback.

2. HARD EXCLUSION CREATES FRAGILE SINGLE-JUDGE RANKINGS:
   In hard exclusion, Aether Engine leaps to Rank #1 purely because 1 judge remains.
   Soft confidence weighting dampens anomalous scores (down to 0.56x and 0.8x)
   while preserving judge consensus, leading to robust, verifiable rank outcomes.
